Live Longer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Live Longer is a personal calorie and weight tracker made by Anthony Leonardi. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. This page explains what the app does with your information.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter (the foods you log, any food photos, your goal, your settings and your Claude API key) is stored only on your iPhone. Your API key is kept in the iPhone's Keychain. The developer never receives any of it.

Your iPhone may include this data in your own iCloud or computer backups, like any other app's data. If you use Settings → Data → Export my data, the backup file goes wherever you choose to save it.

Apple Health

With your permission, Live Longer reads these from Apple Health:

  • weight
  • active and resting calories burned
  • height, date of birth and sex (to estimate your daily calorie needs)

It uses them only on your iPhone, to show your weight trend and set your daily target. Live Longer never writes to Apple Health, and Health data is never sent to the developer, to Anthropic or to anyone else. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Claude (Anthropic)

If you add your own Claude API key, the app sends a request to Anthropic's API when you ask it to estimate a food. The request contains:

  • what you typed or said, or the photo you took
  • the names and calories of your favorite and frequently logged foods, so estimates match how you usually eat
  • the estimation guidance you wrote in Settings

It does not contain your Health data, your weight or your name. Anthropic processes these requests under its own terms and privacy policy, and they are billed to your own Anthropic account. Without a key, nothing is sent to Anthropic.

Voice, camera and Siri

  • Voice: when you dictate a food, Apple's speech recognition turns it into text. Depending on your iPhone, this may be processed by Apple. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Camera: a food photo is kept with that entry on your iPhone (so it can be retried if the estimate fails) and is not saved to your photo library.
  • Siri: the "Log food in Live Longer" command is handled by Siri under Apple's privacy policy.

Widgets

The home screen widgets read a short summary (today's calories, target and colors) that the app saves on your iPhone. It never leaves the device.

Children

Live Longer is not meant for children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. To remove Health permissions, use the Health app.
